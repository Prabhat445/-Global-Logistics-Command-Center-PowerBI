# 🌍 Global Logistics Command Center — Real-Time Shipment & Freight Analytics

An enterprise-grade Power BI dashboard engineered to provide real-time visibility into global shipment costs, freight weight distributions, hub routing efficiencies, and carrier performance.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary
Managing cross-border logistics requires tight control over shipping routes, freight expenditure, and weight metrics. This Command Center aggregates raw supply chain data into strategic operational metrics, helping logistics managers identify high-cost destination hubs and optimize route capacity.

Designed & Developed by: **Prabhat Tiwari** (*Data Analytics & Automation Consultant*)

---

## 📊 Key KPIs & Metrics Tracked
- **Total Freight Cost:** Real-time visibility into total logistics spends ($10.45K).
- **Total & Average Freight Weight:** Tracks operational cargo weight metrics ($4K KG Total Weight / 515.00 Avg Weight).
- **Destination Hub Breakdown:** Direct financial tracking across hubs (Mumbai Hub, Kolkata Hub, Hyderabad Hub, Bengaluru Hub).
- **Route Weight Distribution:** Route-by-route cargo load line analytics for transport optimization.
- **Shipment ID Analysis:** Granular freight distribution by individual Shipment IDs (SHP-101 through SHP-108).
- **Time-Based Cargo Trends:** Breakdown of average shipment weights across Years, Quarters, Months, and Days.
- **Origin Hub Performance:** Visual breakdown of primary shipment origin hubs (Chennai Hub, Delhi Hub, Mumbai Hub).

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Analytical Capabilities
- **Business Intelligence Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- **Data Modeling & Calculations:** Custom DAX (Dynamic Freight Costing, Average Load Metrics, Time Intelligence Functions)
- **Data Transformation:** Power Query / ETL Pipeline
- **Visual Analytics:** Interactive KPI Cards, Horizontal Bar Charts, Treemaps, Pie/Donut Visuals, and Clustered Column Charts
- **Interactivity:** Dynamic Slicers for Destination and Origin Hub filtering.

---

## 💡 Business Value & Key Insights
1. **Cost Minimization by Destination:** Instantly spotlights top shipping spend hubs to negotiate better regional carrier tariffs.
2. **Cargo Capacity Optimization:** Monitors average cargo weights per route to prevent under-utilized or overloaded freight trips.
